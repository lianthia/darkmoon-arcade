MC_DESC	Lead heroes of Azeroth from bounty to bounty: six mercenaries, three at a time, every ability resolved by speed.
MC_TAGLINE	Heroes for hire, bounties across Azeroth
MC_RULES	Pick a bounty at the travel board: every zone ends with its boss, and you choose the path there. Fights put three of your six mercenaries on the board. Each turn you choose an ability for every one of them, then all abilities of both sides resolve by speed - the lowest number goes first. Protectors (red) deal double damage to fighters (green), fighters to casters (blue), casters to protectors. Fallen mercenaries stay down until a spirit healer brings them back. After every won fight one mercenary takes a treasure for the rest of the bounty. Bosses drop coins to recruit and upgrade mercenaries and unlock equipment; beating a zone opens its heroic mode. Foes above your level miss you less and you miss them more - level up before you take on harder zones. Your progress is saved after every step, even in the middle of a fight.

MC_HELP_OVERVIEW_TITLE	How a bounty works
MC_HELP_OVERVIEW	Mercenaries is played in bounties. At the |cff8a4a10Travel Point|r you pick a zone; every zone ends with its boss.\n\n1. Your party of six mercenaries travels a path of stops from the bottom of the map to the boss at the top. At every fork you choose the way.\n2. Fights put three of your mercenaries on the board against the foes.\n3. After every won fight a mercenary takes a treasure that stays with it until the bounty ends.\n4. Beat the boss to finish the bounty: you get coins and possibly new equipment.\n\nBetween bounties you level, recruit and upgrade mercenaries in the |cff8a4a10Mercenaries|r book. Everything is saved after every step - you can close the game in the middle of a fight and continue later.\n\n|cff8a4a10Achievements|r (the task board by the campfire) reward bosses, zones, collections and daring plays such as winning with casters only. Progress you made before an achievement existed counts as well.
MC_HELP_ZONES_TITLE	Bounties and zones
MC_HELP_ZONES	Every zone has the level range of its classic zone. Its boss stands at about two thirds of that range, the foes on the way a little lower.\n\nAll zones are open from the start, but level matters a lot: every level a foe is above your mercenaries makes you miss more often and deal less damage. Foes ten or more levels above you show a skull - such a bounty cannot be won.\n\nThe level next to each foe is coloured like in the game: grey and green are easy, yellow is even, orange and red are dangerous.\n\n|cff8a4a10Heroic:|r once you beat a zone's boss, its heroic mode opens. All foes there are level 60 and much stronger - this is the endgame. Heroic bosses drop more coins and the best equipment.
MC_HELP_PATH_TITLE	The path
MC_HELP_PATH	The coins on the bounty map are the stops. Lit coins are the stops you can travel to next; click one to see what waits there, then press the big button.\n\n|cff8a4a10Fight|r - an ordinary group of foes.\n|cff8a4a10Elite|r - four stronger foes, but the treasure afterwards is better.\n|cff8a4a10Spirit healer|r - brings one fallen mercenary back.\n|cff8a4a10Blessing|r - all mercenaries of one role get more attack and health for the rest of the bounty.\n|cff8a4a10Mystery|r - shows itself on arrival (see Treasures and events).\n|cff8a4a10Boss|r - the end of the bounty.\n\nVisited stops carry a check mark. Stops you can no longer reach fade out. You can abandon a bounty at any time; levels and coins earned so far are kept.
MC_HELP_BATTLE_TITLE	The battle
MC_HELP_BATTLE	A fight starts by choosing which three mercenaries take the board; the others wait on the bench below.\n\nEvery turn has two parts:\n1. |cff8a4a10Orders:|r select each of your mercenaries and pick one of its abilities (cards in the tray). If the ability needs a target, click the target - a line shows whom you aim at. Then press |cff8a4a10Ready!|r\n2. |cff8a4a10Resolution:|r all abilities of both sides run in order of their speed, the |cff8a4a10lowest number first|r. You only learn what the foes do when it happens; the numbers above the portraits show the order.\n\nSome abilities have a cooldown and are not available every turn. When a mercenary falls, you send in a new one from the bench at the start of the next turn. The fight is won when no foe is left. From turn 30 on everybody loses more and more health each turn, so no fight lasts forever.\n\n|cff8a4a10Play turn|r (key R) picks for everyone and starts the turn at once; key A only picks for everyone without starting. At the top right you switch the speed and open the battle log, which lists every hit, heal and defeat.\n\nWhile a turn plays, the tray shows its |cff8a4a10timeline|r: who acts with what, in order, the current action lit up. When you aim at a foe, the damage to expect appears over it (without critical hits), with "Advantage!" if the role circle doubles it. The battle header has buttons for speed, the battle log and the sound.
MC_HELP_ROLES_TITLE	Roles and levels
MC_HELP_ROLES	Every mercenary and foe has a role, shown by the colour of its frame:\n\n|cffc03020Protector (red)|r - tough, protects its friends.\n|cff2f8a20Fighter (green)|r - strong attacks against single foes.\n|cff2050c0Caster (blue)|r - spells, often against several foes.\n\nThe roles beat each other in a circle: |cffc03020Protectors|r deal double damage to |cff2f8a20fighters|r, |cff2f8a20fighters|r to |cff2050c0casters|r and |cff2050c0casters|r to |cffc03020protectors|r. Advantage shows as a bigger number in the fight. A good party mixes all three roles and sends the right mercenary against the right foe.\n\nThe shield on a portrait shows attack, the drop health. Levels raise both, as do equipment, treasures and blessings. Neutral foes (grey) have no strength and no weakness.\n\nEvery portrait also shows its role's |cff8a4a10symbol|r: a shield, crossed swords or a staff. Hover a portrait to see whom it beats and who beats it. Red and green hard to tell apart? Options, Mercenaries, colour-blind friendly role colours.
MC_HELP_EFFECTS_TITLE	Effects
MC_HELP_EFFECTS	|cff8a4a10Taunt|r - foes must attack this unit first.\n|cff8a4a10Stealth|r - cannot be targeted until it acts.\n|cff8a4a10Stun|r - loses its next action.\n|cff8a4a10Divine shield|r (golden glow) - the next hit does no damage.\n|cff8a4a10Absorb|r (number above health) - soaks damage before health.\n|cff8a4a10Damage over time / healing over time|r - works at the end of every turn.\n|cff8a4a10Buffs and debuffs|r - change attack, damage dealt or taken, or speed for a few turns.\n|cff8a4a10Thorns|r - attackers take damage back.\n\nSmall icons on the lower edge of a portrait show what is active. Hover a portrait for its abilities, their values and cooldowns.
MC_HELP_MERCS_TITLE	Improving mercenaries
MC_HELP_MERCS	|cff8a4a10Experience:|r every mercenary of the party gains experience from every won fight, up to level 60.\n|cff8a4a10Abilities:|r the second ability is learned at level 10, the third at level 30.\n|cff8a4a10Coins:|r each mercenary has its own coins. Every won fight gives each party member one coin, a beaten boss more. Each zone's boss also pays coins to certain mercenaries - that is how you recruit new ones.\n|cff8a4a10Recruiting:|r 50 coins of that mercenary.\n|cff8a4a10Ranks:|r abilities can be raised to rank II (30 coins) and rank III (80 coins).\n|cff8a4a10Equipment:|r each mercenary has three pieces. The first is there from the start, the second drops from a zone's boss in normal mode, the third in heroic mode. Hover a piece to see where it drops; click it to equip it.\n\nYour party is set in the book: click a slot on the right, then a mercenary.
MC_HELP_TREASURES_TITLE	Treasures and events
MC_HELP_TREASURES	|cff8a4a10Treasures:|r after a won fight a mercenary may take one of three treasures. It keeps it until the bounty ends. Every treasure has a rarity, as in WoW: common (white), uncommon (green), rare (blue), epic (purple) and legendary (orange). The rarer, the stronger - and the less often it shows up. Elite fights and heroic mode offer rarer treasures; legendary ones only appear there.\n|cff8a4a10Cursed treasures|r are very strong, but each one lowers the health of the whole party for the rest of the bounty.\n\nMysteries:\n|cff8a4a10Stranger|r - copies a treasure one of your mercenaries already carries.\n|cff8a4a10Sabotage|r - the foes of the next fight start with less health.\n|cff8a4a10Portal|r - lets you skip a stop.\n|cff8a4a10Cursed chest|r - a choice of cursed treasures.\n|cff8a4a10Bonus loot|r - the boss pays half as many coins again.\n|cff8a4a10Recruit|r - a mercenary you may not own yet joins as a guest for the rest of the bounty.\n\n|cff8a4a10Score:|r every won fight scores ten times the foes' level; elites score half again, bosses three times as much and heroic bounties double.
