### Darkmoon Arcade 1.4.1

#### Mercenaries
- **More enchants:** 34 enchanting formulas from WoW: Forever join Classic's, among them the Grand Crusader line, Insight, Recovery and Revelation for weapons, Spellblasting and Mighty Spell Power for two-handers, and new ones for bracers, chest, cloaks, shields, off-hands and gloves (nature, arcane and holy power). Like every formula they are found as loot
- **Rewards for stories:** the last task of every chain now also gives an item that suits the mercenary – from the chain's own dungeon where it ends in one. A mercenary that owns it already gets its enchanting materials instead

#### Fixes
- The formula picker on an item showed only six formulas; it now turns pages (arrows beside "Back" or the mouse wheel)
- The version in the launcher read "vv1.4.0"; it shows "v1.4.1" now
