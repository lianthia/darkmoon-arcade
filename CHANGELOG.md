### Darkmoon Arcade 1.6.0 – Goblin Bridge Architect

A new game joins the arcade and takes the spotlight: Goblin Bridge Architect, a physics puzzle about building bridges on a goblin's budget.

#### New game: Goblin Bridge Architect
- **Build and test:** drag beams from joint to joint – road, wood, steel and rope – then send the vehicle across. Every beam feels its load, glows from yellow to red and snaps when it is too much
- **24 contracts in three chapters:** the gorges of Kalimdor, the Eastern Kingdoms and the wild south – from the Barrens to Duskwood at night, a lava river in the Burning Steppes, snowy Winterspring, Tanaris, Un'Goro and Feralas
- **13 vehicles:** from the gnome mechanostrider and the pack mule to the rocket car, the harvest golem and the spider tank, each with its own weight and speed
- **Stars:** one within the budget, two or three for cheaper bridges. Every three-star mark is proven reachable – it just takes a clever bridge
- **Weather and wildlife:** drifting clouds, rain, snow, embers over the lava and fireflies at night; now and then a wyvern, a bat or a hippogryph flies past
- **Comfort:** reach ring while dragging, beam info on hover, undo, double speed (F), the highest load in the result, and help right in the contracts menu
- **15 achievements:** from the first contract to all 72 stars, plus a few odd ones – a bridge of nothing but road and rope, a close call at 95% load or a contract finished during a flight
- **Languages:** English, German, French, Spanish, Russian and Chinese

#### Launcher
- Goblin Bridge Architect is the new game in the spotlight, with its own teaser art, its stars, contracts, achievements and playing time; in the list on the left it follows Mercenaries

### Darkmoon Arcade 1.5.0 – The First Raid

Mercenaries gets its first raid: Upper Blackrock Spire, with ten mercenaries, five against five and bosses that fight in their own ways. Together with it come camp tasks, a fifth rank and a heroic raid for those who want more.

#### Mercenaries: Upper Blackrock Spire
- **The first raid:** seven bosses from Pyroguard Emberseer to General Drakkisath, with Classic's loot – among it the first epic items. Its entrance is a wing of Blackrock Mountain; portals that lead into a raid show a black dragon on the map
- **Ten go, five fight:** choosing the raid opens a page of its own. Your party always goes, and you add up to four reinforcements from your mercenaries (the choice is kept). Fights are five against five, and all ten can step in from the bench
- **Bosses with their own ways:** they stand as in the original raid – some alone (and far stronger for it), some with companions
  - Rend rides Gyth and only comes down when the drake falls
  - The Beast grows angrier every turn from turn 6
  - Drakkisath calls an elite guard every third turn
  - Every boss makes an entrance with its portrait, a line of its own and its own sound; the adventure guide names each boss's way
- **After a boss:** every fallen boss raises the whole raid – there is no spirit healer. Rend's fall grants the Warchief's Blessing: +15% attack and health until the raid ends. The map's title counts the bosses (Boss 3/7)
- **Hard on purpose:** a party with ranks and dungeon items wins about one raid in six or seven, a fully geared one about one in four
- **Heroic raid:** after the first clear – stronger foes, bosses with one more ability, loot half as often again. Two new achievements wait for those who conquer the raid in normal and in heroic mode

#### Mercenaries: more
- **Camp tasks:** tasks for the whole camp, shown first on the task board. The Seal of Ascension opens the raid once any mercenary reaches level 55; everyone in the party counts for it and is paid
- **Rank V:** a fifth rank for abilities, for 250 coins and the raid cleared with the mercenary
- **Help:** a chapter of its own on raids; the chapter on mercenaries names rank V
- **Roadmap:** "The first raid" is done; more raids such as Molten Core and Naxxramas are next

#### Fixes
- Mercenaries' levels read "Level" in German and "stage" in Chinese in some places; they now read "Stufe" and "级"
- The speed on a chosen ability's bubble is clear again (its outline smeared the small number)
