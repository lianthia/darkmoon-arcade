### Darkmoon Arcade 1.6.1

#### New option
- **Stay open in combat:** a new option under Window keeps the arcade open when a fight starts, and `/arcade` opens it in combat too. During the fight your keys go to the game, so moving and casting always work; the mouse keeps playing. Off by default. Many thanks to Deklatis on CurseForge for the idea!

#### Options
- **More addons from us:** the last page of the options introduces our other addon for WoW: Forever, the Forever QoL Panel – with a CurseForge link to copy if you would like to try it
- **Mercenaries** has its own page in the options again
