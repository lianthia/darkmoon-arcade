### Darkmoon Arcade 1.4.0 Alpha – Quests & Dungeons

A preview of 1.4.0 "Quests & Dungeons". This build brings the new arcade; the quests and dungeons for Mercenaries follow in the next builds (see below).

#### Darkmoon Arcade
- **A new launcher:** your games on the left, Mercenaries in the spotlight with your progress, and the most recently updated games on the start page; "Game statistics" in the spotlight leads to its statistics
- **Favorites:** mark games with the star and find them under Favorites; "All games" lists every game
- **Navigation while playing:** in a game the navigation shrinks to its icons and opens when you rest the mouse on it
- **Now on retail too:** the arcade runs on WoW: Forever and on retail. Mercenaries plays the same on both; on retail it shows retail's maps and creature models
- **The bell** shows your latest achievements from all games, with a badge counting the new ones
- **Flights** can open a chosen game, your favorites, or the game you played last (Options > Flights)
- **What's new keeps a history:** every version from 1.3.0 on, missed updates are marked; after an update the window opens on this page
- **Statistics** for the arcade, Mercenaries and every minigame, with play time per game
- **Options** sorted into categories: General, Window, Sound, Flights, Mercenaries, Minigames
- **See-through while moving** (Options > Window): the window fades while you walk or ride; fully visible under the mouse and on flights
- **Minimap button:** left-click opens the launcher, right-click a menu with What's new, Statistics and Options
- **New logo and icon,** drawn by hand

#### Mercenaries
- **A new camp:** your party sits round the campfire, and the camp's places lead on – as in Hearthstone. Each place shows its name when the camp opens and under the mouse
- **Spell effects in battle:** fireballs, frostbolts and lightning fly from caster to target; hits, heals, shields, taunts and stuns show the game's own effects (Options > Mercenaries)
- **Levels matter more:** foes above your level miss less, hit harder and take less damage; all zones retuned
- **New role gems** on tabs, cards and portraits, grey for mercenaries not yet recruited, with a gold fighter gem for the colour-blind option
- The "!" in the camp now also shows when a mercenary can rank up an ability, on the role tabs and on the mercenary's card too
- Roadmap: "Tasks and stories" is next; "More polish" moved back
- Mercenaries fills the whole window

#### Minigames
- The minigames are scaled up to the window's height; Help and Achievements now sit at the foot of each game's sidebar

#### Coming in 1.4.0 – not in this build yet
- **Quests and stories:** a quest chain for each mercenary, close to their lore, rewarded with coins, new mercenaries and unlocks
- **Dungeons:** the Deadmines and the Wailing Caverns. A fixed path where every boss must fall, hidden bosses unlocked by quests (e.g. Mutanus the Devourer), normal and heroic
- **Items and loot tables:** real Classic loot from these dungeons, bound to the mercenary who gets it; one item slot, a second at the highest level. The adventure guide shows each boss's loot with its drop chance
- **Enchanting:** disenchant items into Classic materials and enchant with recipes found as loot; the strongest enchants need the hardest content
- **Rank IV:** a fourth rank for abilities, for coins plus a dungeon cleared with that mercenary
